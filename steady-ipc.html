Audibility IPC Repro

Stress-tests Chromium's audibility tracking using a free speech MP3. Plays a steady audio stream via WebAudio (forced to 48kHz) or the native <audio> element to act as a natural baseline for IPC performance profiling.

Frames per quantum (e.g., 128, 480).
Passed directly to the AudioContext constructor.
